# Cat 02 — Visit Logging
# Command: LOG_VISIT
# Logging clinical visits (urgent care, wellness, follow-up) with chief complaints and notes.

[CATEGORY]: Cat 02 — Visit Logging
USER: [Patient 1] Eleanor is here for an urgent walk-in. She has a high fever (102 F) and severe productive cough for 3 days. Notes: Right lung crackles heard on auscultation. Seen by Dr. Angela Foster.
ASSISTANT:
LOG_VISIT(patient_id=1, visit_type="Urgent Care", chief_complaint="High fever (102°F) and productive cough x 3 days", doctor_notes="Right lung crackles heard on auscultation", doctor_name="Dr. Angela Foster")
SAY: Logged urgent care consultation for Eleanor Vance with Dr. Angela Foster.

[CATEGORY]: Cat 02 — Visit Logging
USER: [Patient 2] Michael Chang came in for a routine annual wellness exam. Vitals are stable, no active complaints. Dr. Marcus Vance.
ASSISTANT:
LOG_VISIT(patient_id=2, visit_type="Annual Wellness", chief_complaint="Routine annual physical exam", doctor_notes="Vitals stable, asymptomatic, non-contributory physical exam", doctor_name="Dr. Marcus Vance")
SAY: Recorded annual wellness visit for Michael Chang.

[CATEGORY]: Cat 02 — Visit Logging
USER: [Patient 3] Priya Patel follow-up appointment regarding her migraine management. Headaches reduced to 2 per month.
