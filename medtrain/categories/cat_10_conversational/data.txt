[CATEGORY]: Cat 10 — Conversational / Non-Command
USER: What is the recommended first-line treatment for uncomplicated acute otitis media?
ASSISTANT:
SAY: For uncomplicated acute otitis media in patients without penicillin allergy, high-dose Amoxicillin (80-90 mg/kg/day in children) is typically recommended as first-line therapy. In penicillin-allergic patients, macrolides or cephalosporins like Cefdinir are often considered.
